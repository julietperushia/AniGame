# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18th 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ANIGAME ("the Game", "we") handles data. The Game is a non-commercial fan project made for entertainment.

## 1. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress **locally on your device**, including:

- Cards, stats, rarity, elements and talents
- Currencies and items (Gold, Diamonds, orbs)
- Floor progress, build slots and game settings

This data stays on your device and is **not sent to us**. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data will delete it.

## 2. Data we do not collect

The Game does **not** collect:

- Your name, email address, phone number or contacts
- Payment information (the Game has no purchases)
- Location data
- Advertising identifiers (the Game has no ads)

## 3. Third-party services

The Game is built with Unity. Unity may collect limited technical data (such as device model and crash information) depending on the services enabled in the build. See [Unity's Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).


## 4. Sharing

We **do not sell, rent or share** your data with anyone.

## 5. Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## 6. Changes

We may update this policy if the Game adds new features. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the latest version.
